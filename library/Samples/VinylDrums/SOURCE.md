# Rhino Vinyl Drums

282 one-shot vinyl drum hits from the *Giant Vinyl Drums Pack*, produced by
Dmitriy "Cyberworm" Vasilyev and published royalty free at <http://www.rhythm-lab.com>.
All files are 44.1 kHz 16-bit WAV, as distributed; some are mono and some stereo.
The audio is byte-identical to the download — only the names and the folders changed.

## Layout

| Folder | Files | Holds |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `Kick/` | 84 | `Kick NN` |
| `Snare/` | 92 | `Snare NN` and `Snare Roll NN` |
| `Hat/` | 68 | `Hat Closed NN`, `Hat Open NN`, `Ride NN` |
| `Tom/` | 17 | `Tom NN` |
| `Clap/` | 9 | `Clap NN` |
| `Percussion/` | 12 | `Bongo NN`, `Rimshot NN`, `Hit NN` |

## Naming

`Type NN Tone Length Variant.wav`. The number is the one the pack shipped with, so
gaps in it (the pack has no `clap05` or `clap06`) are the source's, not a mistake.
`Variant` is the pack's own word for the hit — `Crash`, `Drive`, `FX` — kept verbatim.

`Tone` and `Length` are measured, not transcribed: every file was decoded and analysed
for its dominant partial below 300 Hz, its spectral centroid over the first 200 ms, and
its decay to -40 dBFS. Thresholds sit on the breaks in this pack's own distributions, so
the words rank hits against each other rather than against any absolute:

| Type | Tone | Length |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Kick | `Sub` `Deep` `Round` `High` by fundamental (30-185 Hz) | `Tight` under 0.22 s, `Long` over 0.50 s |
| Snare | `Dark` `Warm` `Crisp` `Bright` by centroid | `Tight` under 0.22 s, `Long` over 0.50 s |
| Hat | `Dark` `Crisp` `Bright` `Sizzle` by centroid | closed: `Tight` under 0.15 s, `Loose` over 0.30 s; open: `Washy` over 0.35 s |
| Tom, Bongo | `Low` `Mid` `High` by fundamental | `Long` over 0.35 s |
| Clap | `Dark` `Crisp` `Bright` by centroid | `Tight` under 0.16 s, `Room` over 0.24 s |
| Rimshot, Hit | `Dark` `Crisp` `Bright` by centroid | `Long` over 0.45 s |

A length word the pack already supplied (`short`, `long`) is kept and the measured one
dropped, so no file carries two.
