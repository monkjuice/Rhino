# Arrangement view

`Arrangement` is the timeline: track cards on the left, clip lanes on the right, the pinned [main track](main-track.md) row and the [time row](#the-time-row) at the foot. It is one class defined across fourteen translation units (as of 2026-10-23), all in `native/src/ui/arrangement/` since 2026-10-13 with `ArrangementGrid.h` and `MonitorSelector.*` beside them ([The source tree mirrors the layers](source-tree-mirrors-the-layers.md)): `Arrangement.cpp` (keys, zoom, split, playhead), `ArrangementGeometry.cpp` (lanes, time to x, snapping, hit zones), `ArrangementPainter.cpp` (rows, grid, rulers, the selection) and `ArrangementClipPainter.cpp` (every clip, piece by piece; split off on 2026-10-23, leaving the painter at 631 lines), `ArrangementSync.cpp` (rebuilding views and card controls from the edit), `ArrangementGestures.cpp` (pointer, card drags, menus, autoscroll, middle-drag pan), `ArrangementSelection.cpp` (region and clipboard), `ArrangementTrackClipboard.cpp` (the clipboard commands on whole cards, [Track clipboard](track-clipboard.md)), `ArrangementAutomation.cpp` and `ArrangementAutomationPaint.cpp`, `ArrangementGroups.cpp`, `ArrangementRename.cpp`, `ArrangementDrops.cpp` and `ArrangementBounce.cpp` (Ctrl+B, [Bounce to New Track](bounce-to-new-track.md)). Shared helpers are in `ArrangementInternal.h`; the clip-edit arithmetic is the pure header `model/ClipGeometry.h`, unit-tested without a `Session`.

## How it stays responsive and correct

- **Preview, then one commit.** A drag edits a local `preview`, and the model's command and its undo transaction run once, on release: `Session::editClip` for a trim and, since 2026-10-23, `Session::moveClipRegion` for a move, because a header press carries the whole selection ([Region editing](region-editing.md#moving-a-selection)). During a move `original` and `preview` are the grabbed piece, the part of the grabbed clip the selection carries or the whole clip, and the selection follows it by the same distance (`displayedTimeSelection`); `snappedClipMoveStart` skips the clips the selection carries. Mid-drag, a change notification only repaints. Arrow nudges go through `moveClipRegion` too and are taken whenever the selection is a range, otherwise reaching the window (commit `d20faa4`); until 2026-10-23 a move and a nudge were `Session::moveClips` over the selected clips (commit `b5d17e0`), whose loop is now the shared `moveClipsInEdit`.
- **Clip views are a cache, kept per track** (2026-10-23). `sync()` rebuilds the `ClipView` list on each change notification, never per paint, but reads a track's clips again (`rebuildTrackClips`) only when its `Session::trackRevision` has moved since or the track is new. A `TrackClipCache` per track id holds the revision, the views, the files their waveforms draw and the sync pass that last kept it; a sync copies the views into `clips` with the track's current index, drops the caches of tracks that are gone, and `editWillChange` clears them all. At 135 tracks and 103,072 notes a sync fell from 11.9 to 2.7 ms ([The 120-track stress audit](stress-audit-2026-10.md)). A command that changes what a clip shows has to mark its track, or the cache keeps the old view ([What a change costs the interface](ui-cost-of-a-change.md)). Waveforms share one thumbnail per source file. A `Session` call announces synchronously, so the list can be rebuilt before the call returns: copy a `ClipView` before calling `Session` if you read it afterwards (commit `2df76f8`). The sync also reads each track's facts once (`TrackFacts`), and the painter skips whatever a repaint does not reach ([What a change costs the interface](ui-cost-of-a-change.md)).
- **A MIDI clip's notes are kept in the sequence's own seconds.** `ClipView::midiNotes` is a `shared_ptr<const std::vector<MidiNoteView>>`, shared with the track's cache, and the painter draws each note at `xFor(note.start + origin)` with `origin = position.start - position.offset`, the origin of the window the clip is onto its sequence. A clip moved or trimmed, and its preview mid-drag, converts nothing, and the old `noteShift` arithmetic is gone. `clipNameWidths`, the measured width of each name, is kept across syncs, cleared past 8,192 entries and with the document.
- **Ids, not pointers.** The selection is a `TimeSelection` rectangle (start, end, first and last track) plus `selected`, the primary clip's `te::EditItemID`; nothing keeps a `te::Clip*`, because one dies on undo or project replacement ([Hold ids, not pointers](ids-not-pointers.md)).
- **`rows` is the vertical authority.** Revealed automation lanes stack extra rows under a track and collapsed group members get zero-height rows, so `lane(track)` looks the track up in `rows` rather than multiplying.

## Rules worth knowing

- **A clip is two rows.** The top strip (`clipHeaderHeight`: half the clip, clamped to 8-22 px) selects the clip's own span or, when the selection already touches the clip, carries the whole selection ([Pointer and selection rules](pointer-and-selection.md)); the body behaves like empty lane, so a press there sets the selection line and a drag sweeps a region. Edges trim at any height, and a trim edits that one clip.
- **A carried selection is drawn in pieces, and marked where it cannot land** (2026-10-23). `displayedPieces` (`ArrangementGeometry.cpp`) yields up to three pieces per clip - the remainder left before the region, the carried piece at its previewed place on its previewed track, the remainder after - from `carriedCut`, which is the model's own `cutClipByRegion` (`model/ClipGeometry.h`); `paintClipPiece` outlines a piece as selected when it is carried or the selection holds the whole clip. `previewRowRefused(row)` predicts from the sync's `TrackFacts` (bus, midi) and `ClipView::midi` (set in `ArrangementSync.cpp`) whether a row's material could land on its destination row, and `paintTimeSelection` draws Live's circle-with-slash (`palette::recordAccent`, 16 px) at the left edge of the carried rectangle on each refused row; the model is the authority on release, and its refusal goes to the status line. `factsFor(track)` answers a default `TrackFacts` for a track past the end, which the preview and the clip painter rely on for a lane the drop would make. The sync no longer derives the region from a set of selected clips, but the selection follows the clip it is exactly: `sync()` asks `isExactly` of the primary clip on the old list before rebuilding, then sets the rectangle to the clip's new span, or clears it when the clip is gone; any other rectangle only has its rows clamped and stays where it was put. A primary clip that has left the list is dropped either way, announcing `clipSelected({})` ([Region editing](region-editing.md#one-selection)).
- **The selection line is where playback starts.** `moveTransportToSelectionStart` always calls `Session::setPlaybackStart`, but moves the transport only while stopped and not counting in.
- **Two snapping rules.** Clip edges round to the nearest line (`snapped`); a pointer position takes the cell it is inside (`snappedDown`/`snappedUp`). The grid is adaptive by default (`ArrangementGrid.h`); see [Zoom and the adaptive grid](#zoom-and-the-adaptive-grid).
- **A new clip fills its grid cell** (commit `3a0933c`). A double-click on an empty lane (`ArrangementGestures.cpp`) and Ctrl+A at the playhead (`Arrangement.cpp`) start it at `snappedDown`, the cell the pointer or playhead is in, and pass `newClipBeats()`: the resolved cell (`resolvedGridBeats()`, triplet included), or 0 with the grid off. `Session::createClip` makes it that long but never under a bar, and ends it at a clip ahead ([Clips never overlap](clip-placement.md)): one bar at the opening zoom, two on a two-bar grid, up to eight. Before, it was always one bar, half a cell on a two-bar grid, and started at `snapped`, so a click in the right half of a long cell made the clip in the next one. `NoteEditor.inc` pins the lengths and the start.
- **The bar ruler labels counts too.** Bar numbers step by powers of two bars, 44 px apart (`barLabelStep`, `barNumberMinimumPixels`). Once they are one bar apart, `paintBarNumbers` also labels counts as `bar.count` with short marks between, like Live. A count is the signature's denominator note (`timeSignature().numerator` per bar, so 6/8 reads 1.2 to 1.6 in eighths), not the engine's quarter-note beat (`beatsPerBar()`). Every span comes from one chain in `ArrangementGrid.h`, `finerRulerSpan`, where each span divides the previous: 4/4 runs 4, 2, 1, 1/2, 1/4; 6/8 runs 6, 3, 1; 7/8 runs 7, 1. That chain is what makes every mark land on a division of the labels either side. `rulerLabelSpan` keeps labels 44 px apart and never goes finer than a count. `rulerTickSpan` keeps marks 10 px apart and stops at a quarter count. The ruler ignores the snap grid. The time row at the foot no longer follows the bar numbers: it reads round times, then the grid ([The time row](#the-time-row)). Unit-tested in `tests/Arrangement/ClipGeometryTest.cpp`.
- **`Focus`** records what the last click selected (`none`, `region`, `track` or `automation`; `Focus::clip` went with the one-selection change of 2026-10-23, a clicked clip being a region), and Delete, Ctrl+B, Ctrl+C, Ctrl+X and Ctrl+D dispatch on it: on `Focus::track` they act on every selected card ([Track clipboard](track-clipboard.md)), otherwise on the region. Ctrl+V follows whichever clipboard was filled last.
- **A muted clip is drawn grey** (since 2026-10-14): `ClipView::muted`, a desaturated body, grey waveform and dim notes in `ArrangementPainter.cpp`. The clip menu's ticked *Mute* (`muteSelected`, `Session::setClipsMuted`) mutes either kind of clip as one undo step; before, a muted audio clip looked like any other and a MIDI clip could not be muted at all.
- **Anything painted on the lanes clips at `headerWidth`** (commit `137ccb5`, 2026-10-24). `xFor` gives true positions, so a clip starting before `viewStart` has a `box` (`boundsOf`) that runs left under the track cards, and `paint()` draws the cards before the clips, so nothing covers a spill. `paintClipPiece` (`ArrangementClipPainter.cpp`) clipped at x 0, the panel's edge, a rule older than the painter's split in `cff657d`: scrolled past a clip's start, its body fill, 4 px top strip and outline were painted across the card's controls and name columns in the clip's colour (the name, notes and waveform were already held to `visible`). Its clip region and `visible` now both run from `headerWidth` to `getWidth() - 14`, as the automation rows, the recording band and `paintTimeSelection` already did; only the row dividers span from x 0 across the cards, deliberately. `Rendering.inc` paints the panel with the view inside the pattern clip and with the clip out of sight, and requires the card column across lane 0 to match pixel for pixel.
- **Autoscroll runs on the frame clock** (`VBlankAttachment`), because a pointer held still past the edge sends no drag events. So do the level meters: each frame runs `autoScrollDrag`, `updateMeters` and `updatePlayhead`, in that order, so the playhead's immediate `UpdateWindow` handoff also collects the meters' damage ([Playhead rendering](playhead.md)).
- **A card reads meter, controls, name** (commits `14c9c77` and `83135f7`, 2026-10-15). The header is `headerWidth` 272 px: a `LevelMeter` at `cardMeterInset` (9) plus the group indent, 8 px of room, the controls column (`cardControlsWidth` 148, its controls from `cardControlLeft` 24 and 116 px wide), a 4 px divider, and the name column, 120 px on the track's colour. Three buttons and their gaps (3 × 36 + 2 × 4) span exactly the two faders and theirs (2 × 56 + 4), so the buttons sit squarely over the faders. The main row reads meter at x 9, MAIN at 24, volume at 70, pan at 136. `updateMeters` is the single reader of `Session::takeOutputLevels`, skips while the arrangement is hidden, and drops the bars to empty on its return ([Track level meters](level-meters.md)).
- **A selected track's whole row is lit** (commit `83135f7`, 2026-10-15). `paintTrackCards` lays `palette::selectedRowWash` over the card and over the lane's ground across the arrangement for every selected track (`isTrackSelected`, the whole multi-selection), under the bar bands, grid lines and clips. It replaces the 3 px `palette::selection` strip at the card's left edge, so nothing now marks which of several selected cards is the working track; the Device View still follows it. The main row's header keeps `palette::sideSurface` and, selected, takes the same wash on its header and lane instead of `palette::hover` and the strip. `TrackManagement.inc` checks it by painting the arrangement twice, row 0 selected and then row 1, and the main row the same way ([Writing Rhino tests](writing-rhino-tests.md)).
- **Every card has a fold arrow, before its name** (commit `14c9c77`). `disclosureBounds(track)` puts it at the start of the name column on the name's line; the name, and its rename editor, start `disclosureGap` after it and end 6 px short of the header edge (`trackNameBounds`). `paintDisclosure` draws it in the name's own colour, pointing at what a click reveals; `beginDisclosureGesture` takes the click. On a bus it collapses or expands the group's members and never folds the bus row ([Track groups](track-groups.md)). On any other card, members included, it folds the row to `minimumLaneHeight` (32 px: the name and the button line) as Live's fold button does, and unfolds it to the height it had: `Session::setTrackFolded` writes `rhinoTrackFolded` (present only while folded, a view setting with no undo, `ModelChange::none`), `laneHeightFor` answers the minimum while it is set and leaves `rhinoLaneHeight` alone, and `setTrackLaneHeight` clears it, so dragging a folded card's bottom edge unfolds it. `TrackFacts::folded` gives the arrow its direction once per sync.
- **Waveforms already fill their height.** `paintWaveformLanes` draws JUCE's `AudioThumbnail` (peaks stored as int8 × 127, drawn at zoom × height / 256) at zoom 1.45 (`ArrangementPainter.cpp`), so a peak of about -3.2 dBFS reaches the lane edge and anything louder is clipped at it. In a default 96 px row the clip box is 86 px, its name strip and padding take 36, and the 50 px left split into two channel lanes of about 25 and 23 px. A waveform that looks small is quiet material: a recording reported as loud (2026-10-15) peaked at -4.4 dBFS but 90% of its pixel columns sat below -12.6 dBFS. Measure the file before changing the drawing ([Measure sound, don't read the DSP](measure-sound-dont-read-dsp.md)).
- **It reports, the shell decides.** `clipSelected`, `clipOpened`, `clipSpanSwept`, `trackSelected` and `trackFocused` are callbacks; which panel opens is the [app shell](app-shell.md)'s call. `clipSpanSwept(clip, start, end)` (2026-10-24) fires when a finished sweep has named a primary clip, which the shell passes to the note editor ([Region editing](region-editing.md#one-selection)). The shell also reads `selectedClipID()` on every session change, not only on `clipSelected`, so changing `selected` mid-gesture is visible to it even unannounced.
- **Cards.** A card's bottom edge resizes its row and its body carries the track through the stack (`Session::moveTrack`), both previewed until release. Row height (`rhinoLaneHeight`, 0 means fit the panel) is a view setting written without undo; colour and name are undoable. Renaming puts one `TextEditor` over the painted name. A track from the `+` menu or Ctrl+T lands under the selected card and becomes the selection (`insertTrack`, see [Track kinds: audio and MIDI](track-kinds.md)).

## Zoom and the adaptive grid

Since 2026-10-23, modelled on Ableton Live but not identical to it (`Arrangement.h`, `Arrangement::zoom`, `ArrangementGrid.h`).

- **It opens on two minutes.** `viewSpan` is seconds across the lane width and starts at `defaultViewSeconds` (120; it was 8). At 120 BPM the adaptive grid reads that in one-bar cells on any lane about 1,360-2,700 px wide (a 1920 or 2560 screen with the browser open). Zoom-out stops at max(2 × `defaultViewSeconds`, 2 × `songEnd`); the old cap, max(60 s, 2 × song), would clamp the first zoom-out from the opening view back to one minute.
- **Zoom-in stops at 1/16384.** The floor is lane width ÷ `deepestZoomPixelsPerBeat` × seconds per beat (it was a fixed 0.25 s). `deepestZoomPixelsPerBeat` is the medium target over a 1/16384, 32 × 4096 = 131,072 px per beat, held per beat so the deepest view shows 1/16384 at every tempo: about 262 px/ms at 120 BPM. That is shallower in time than Live: a screenshot at Live's maximum zoom shows a single whole-millisecond reading across about 975 px, so Live reaches at least about 650 px/ms. Live's grid, though, appears to stop at 1/8192: it draws 1/8192 lines 14 px apart at one zoom and still 28 px apart at a deeper one, where it would otherwise have halved them.
- **The adaptive grid picks the division nearest its target.** `adaptiveGridDivision` takes the finest division whose lines are at least target ÷ √2 apart (22.6 px at medium), which for divisions an octave apart is the nearest one. The old rule, finest that clears the whole target, often read one division coarser: over two minutes at 120 BPM it needed a 1,920 px lane for one-bar cells where the new rule needs about 1,360, and at the zoom of one Live screenshot it chose 1/4096 lines 56 px apart where Live drew 1/8192 lines 28 px apart. Live draws denser still: one-bar cells 20 px apart (two minutes on a ~1,157 px lane), 1/8192 lines 14 px apart. The √2 rule was kept because at Live's density two minutes across the user's ~2,030 px lane would read in half bars, and the user asked for one bar there. The five targets (72/48/32/20/12 px) are unchanged.
- **Divisions run past 1/64**, `note128` to `note16384`. The Fixed menu lists only the first `fixedGridDivisionCount` (10, through 1/64), as Live's stops early; the finer ones come from the adaptive grid and Ctrl+1, since `narrowerGridDivision` walks the whole chain.
- **A grid too fine to draw is drawn coarser; snapping keeps it.** `drawnGridBeats` steps a fixed or off-mode grid whose lines fall under `gridLineMinimumPixels` (4 px) out to the counts, then the bars, and since commit `cf4e959` draws bars still that close every other bar, then every fourth, and so on, as a fixed one-bar grid across a long song needs. Drawn line for line, 1/64 across two minutes is about 3,840 lines, past the painter's 2,000-line cap, which left the right of the view bare.
- **The adaptive grid still reaches eight bars.** The user briefly asked to cap it at one bar, then kept multi-bar cells because songs of eight minutes and more need them (2026-10-23).

Covered by `tests/Arrangement/ClipGeometryTest.cpp` (the nearest rule, the deepest zoom reaching `note16384`, `drawnGridBeats` and its bar thinning) and the scenario `BrowserDrops.inc` (one-bar opening at lane widths 1673 and 2313, zoom-out widening it, nothing past 1/16384).

**Open at deep zoom** (as of 2026-10-23):

- The bar ruler still stops, labels at counts and marks at a quarter count (`rulerFinestSpan`), so at deep zoom the top ruler is empty; Live's keeps fine ticks there. The [time row](#the-time-row) reads at every zoom.
- Waveforms are `AudioThumbnail` peaks, blocky rather than single samples below thumbnail resolution.
- `xFor` returns float: a clip starting minutes before a deep view, tens of millions of pixels off-screen, loses a few pixels of edge precision, and past about 2^31 px (about 2.3 hours at 120 BPM at the deepest zoom) an integer conversion would overflow.
- The 92 px toolbar grid button squeezes "Grid 1/16384" and its arrow to about 86% width (79% with T), inside JUCE's 0.7 floor.

## The time row

The strip at the foot, above the horizontal scrollbar, reads wall-clock time: `paintTimeRuler` in `ArrangementPainter.cpp`, its steps in `ArrangementGrid.h`, its text in `ui/shared/WallClock.h`. Since commit `61822a1` (2026-10-23) it reads round times, as Live's does, and readings on the grid where round times run out, which the user accepted. Before, it stepped by whole bars so that every time stood under a bar number (commit `baf0d38`); at 120 BPM that jumped 2 s at a time at a 1/32 grid and printed nothing at 1/512 or deeper, where no bar starts inside the view. `timeTicksPerLabel` is gone; `barLabelStep` and `firstLabelledBar` now serve the bar numbers alone.

- **Round times.** `roundTimeSteps`, in microseconds: 100, 200 and 500 µs (marks only), 1, 2 and 5 × 10^k ms, 1, 2, 5, 10, 15 and 30 s, and 1, 2, 5, 10, 15, 30 and 60 min. `roundTimeLabelStep(pixelsPerSecond)` takes the finest of 1 ms or more that leaves `timeLabelMinimumPixels` (88 px) between readings; `roundTimeTickStep` marks the gap with the finest round step that cuts it into at most `timeTickDivisions` (8), at least `timeTickMinimumPixels` (20 px) apart. Whole seconds and minutes print `mm:ss` (00:10), finer steps `mm:ss:mmm`. On a 2,027 px lane at 120 BPM: two minutes read 00:00, 00:10 ... 01:50, a 1/32 view every 200 ms, a 1/512 view every 20 ms.
- **Then the grid.** `timeRowReadsGrid(gridReadingSeconds)` hands the row to the grid's own divisions once they read at least twice as often as whole milliseconds (0.5 ms apart or less); any sooner would trade round readings for ones barely closer. `timeLabelSpan(pixelsPerCount, countsPerBar)` walks the bar ruler's chain (`finerRulerSpan`) on below the counts, floored only at 1e-6 counts, and `timeTickSpan` marks down the same chain, so every reading sits on a grid line. Readings carry `clockDecimalsForStep` decimals of a millisecond (none at 100 ms apart or more, one more per tenfold closer, at most 3, so three in practice), printed by `formatClock(seconds, withMilliseconds, millisecondDecimals)`. The deepest zoom at 120 BPM reads 00:01:300.293, 00:01:300.781 ..., 0.488 ms apart. The hand-over comes at about 180 px/ms at 120 BPM; at 40 BPM never, the deepest zoom still reading round milliseconds.
- **Marks count from the start of the song** (time 0, or count 0 on the grid), not from the view's edge, so the labelled ones do not shuffle on scroll. The paint loop caps the marks it paints (4096), not the mark's index: inside a deeply zoomed bar the first visible index is far past 4096.
- **Tests.** `ClipGeometryTest.cpp` sweeps the round steps, the grid chain, the hand-over, the decimals and `formatClock`, and at 120 BPM in 4/4 keeps readings at least 88 px and under 2.5 × 88 px apart from hours down to the deepest zoom, a 1/32 view reading every 500 ms or closer and a 1/512 view every 20 ms or closer. `BrowserDrops.inc` paints the row at four zooms and requires text between bars at 1/512 and at the deepest zoom; with `RHINO_TIME_ROW_SNAPSHOT=<path.png>` set, the arrangement workflow under ctest writes the four rows stacked (two minutes, 1/32, 1/512, deepest; [Seeing the UI without taking the screen](headless-ui-snapshots.md)).

## Clips that leave the panel

A clip dragged up out of the panel by more than the band where the lanes scroll (24 px above the top) stops being a move (one clip leaves, the primary selection, whatever else is selected): `Arrangement::mouseDrag` cancels the gesture, so the clip stays put, and starts a window-wide drag carrying `rhino-clip:<id>` (`ClipDrag.h`) with a small tile for a picture. The DJ toggle in the control bar shows the consoles while that drag hovers it, and a console's clips grid takes a copy. The lanes take the reverse: a console's cell dropped on a lane is copied there at the drop point, held to the lane's kind (`ArrangementDrops.cpp`, `rhino-slot:`). See [DJ view and the booth](dj-view.md).
