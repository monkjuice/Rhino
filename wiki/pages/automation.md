# Track automation

Automation belongs to a track and spans the whole timeline; it moved off clips in September 2026. Its clip-local counterpart, since 2026-10-26, is an audio clip's own Gain, Pan and Pitch envelopes. These are kept and edited apart from track lanes, but mirrored by the same pass ([Clip envelopes](clip-envelopes.md)). The model is `native/src/model/SessionAutomation.cpp`; the arrangement's lane rows, hit testing and gestures are `ArrangementAutomation.cpp`, the drawing `ArrangementAutomationPaint.cpp`, the card's chooser `ArrangementAutomationChooser.cpp`, the fader and pan menu `ArrangementMixerAutomation.cpp`.

## Storage

A lane is a `rhinoTrackAutomation` child of its track's state (the edit's state for the main track), with `device`, `parameter` (the rack's exposed-parameter index) and `ownLane`, holding `point` children with `time` in **seconds** and `value`. It rides the project snapshot. A lane keeps no copy of its track index, because deleting a track renumbers every track below.

**A lane names its device by key, not by slot** (commit `cf85e3f`). `device` holds the plugin's `rhinoDeviceKey`, a UUID that `ensureDeviceKey` (`SessionInternal.cpp`) writes onto the plugin's state the first time a lane needs one. It is written without undo, because naming a device is not an edit to it, and it is saved and restored with the device. The slot is worked out from the key whenever lanes are read (`slotOfDevice`), so a lane follows its device through a drag along the chain, a device added in front of it or one deleted beside it. A stored slot used to leave the lane driving whichever device moved into that place. Deleting a device, or replacing a track's instrument, deletes its lanes in the same undo step (`removeDeviceLanes`). A document saved with slot-indexed lanes loads without them ([No backward compatibility for .rhinoedit](no-rhinoedit-back-compat.md)).

A lane with fewer than two points is revealed but inert: a dotted line at the knob's resting value that drives nothing. That is what makes *Show automation* safe.

## Reaching it

Right-click a knob in the Device View (`DeviceEditorPanel::showParameterMenu`): *Show automation* draws the lane over the track, *Show automation on new lane* stacks a dimmed copy of the track underneath, *Hide* removes the lane, *Delete* clears its points. On a lane, a click adds a node and starts dragging it; a double-click removes one.

### The card's fader and pan

Since commit `fd03386` (2026-10-27), at the user's request, a right-click on a track card's volume or pan bar opens the same menu, plus *Re-enable automation* once the bar has been moved by hand (`showMixerMenu`, `ui/arrangement/ArrangementMixerAutomation.cpp`; tests call the seam `chooseMixerAutomation`). The bars are `MixerSlider`s, which swallow the popup-menu press ([A juce::Button fires its click on a right-click too](juce-button-fires-on-right-click.md#sliders-too)).

- **It is an ordinary lane.** `Session::trackMixerTarget(track, MixerControl::volume|pan)` (`SessionMixer.cpp`) names the track's own `te::VolumeAndPanPlugin` by its raw slot, and the parameter by its index among the plugin's active automatable parameters, found by pointer rather than assumed (0 Volume, 1 Pan at the pinned Tracktion commit). The fader then gets a device key like any device, so the lane is stored, mirrored, saved, renewed on a track paste and rescaled by tempo with no case of its own.
- **Named "Mixer"** (Live's name) by `readTrackAutomations`, because `deviceSlots` hides the channel strip and would leave the name blank; the chooser lists "Mixer: Volume" and "Mixer: Pan".
- **Its values are the engine's fader position**, 0 to 1 on Tracktion's fader law (-inf to +6 dB), not the card's -60 to +6 dB. Readouts print dB through the parameter's `valueToString`.
- **Not reachable:** the main row (its bars are plain `juce::Slider`s, so a right-click there still moves the level, and `trackMixerTarget` is invalid because the master volume plugin is not in the master plugin list) and the sends (an `AuxSendPlugin` knob could take the same route).

The end of `AutomationLanes.inc` covers the target, a right-press leaving the level alone, the naming and dB readout, the engine curve, the follow list, override and re-enable, the pan on its own row, delete and hide. The popup itself is not exercised.

## The automation chooser

Since 2026-10-25 (commit `92b4258`), at the user's request: they could not edit automation drawn over a track. A track's own row used to be read-only for automation unless the toolbar's global **A** button (`automationButton`, "automation edit mode") was on, and then it drew every lane over the track and edited the nearest curve. The A button and the mode are gone; the grid button took its slot (`toolbarGrid` at x 124).

- **The card.** Every track card, a group bus and a return included, has `automationChooser`, a menu button in the second fader column of the third control line, right of the input chooser, shown from `inputLaneHeight` (72 px) up ([Arrangement view](arrangement-view.md)). It reads the shown lane's parameter name or "None", drawn crisp and elided by the same `ChooserButton` as the input chooser ([Arrangement view](arrangement-view.md)). Its menu (`showAutomationChooser`) lists None and every lane drawn over the track as "Device: Parameter"; a lane on a row of its own is left out, being edited there. The card's right-click menu has *Automation: <name>...* for a row too short for the chooser. Tests call `showAutomationOnTrack`, the seam the popup's callback goes through.
- **The model.** `Session::setTrackShownAutomation(track, lane)` writes `rhinoShownAutomation` on the track state: the lane's device key, `/`, its parameter index (by key, for the reason a lane names its device by key), or `none` for an invalid lane. It is a view setting like the two folds, written without undo and marked `ModelChange::none`, and refuses the main track and a lane on a row of its own. `readTrackAutomations` resolves it to `TrackAutomation::shown`: the named lane while it is still drawn over the track, otherwise the first lane that is (a chooser never touched, an older document, a lane hidden or undone away), nothing for `none`.
- **A lane put over the track becomes the shown one.** `ensureTrackAutomationState` writes the choice, outside the undo step, whenever it makes a lane over the track or moves one onto it, so *Show automation* and drawing on a parameter with no lane yet are editable at once. **A new way of revealing a lane over a track must go through it**, or the lane lands behind whichever one the row shows.
- **The row.** A track's row draws only its shown lane (`paintAutomationRow`) and edits it with no mode. Its curve area (`automationArea`) starts under the strip the clip names are on and ends 8 px off the row's foot. That keeps the strip for the clips (select, carry, trim, double-click for a clip) and lets the body below edit the curve, Live's automation-mode split. The top is measured from the clip band, as a clip box is: `clipBandOf(row)` top + `clipHeaderHeight` + 3 px. Until commit `81a898b` (2026-10-27) the area repeated the boxes' old 5 px inset. Now that clips fill the row, the curve starts higher (by 5 px on a default 96 px row; [Arrangement view](arrangement-view.md#rules-worth-knowing)). Above the curve only a node within grab radius answers. A row too short for an 8 px curve (`minimumCurveHeight`), such as one folded to its one line, shows none; on None the row is all clips, and every lane still plays.
- **Focus.** `sync()` drops automation focus from a lane no longer drawn anywhere (hidden, or over its track while the row shows another), so Delete never clears an invisible curve.

## Dragging a level

Since 2026-10-25 (commit `92b4258`): the user found vertical steps too coarse. A lane row is 44 px with a 30 px curve (a track row's about 24-58 px), so following the pointer absolutely moved about 3% per pixel, and the 5 px snap onto the neighbours' line held a node across a sixth of a short lane's range.

- **By travel, not position.** `dragAutomationGesture` moves the level by how far the pointer travels: the whole range takes `automationDragTravel` (200 px), or the curve's height where that is taller, so a tall row still feels 1:1. Shift divides each step by `automationFineDivisor` (10). The level is carried and clamped step by step (`automationDragValue`, `automationDragY`), so a node pushed past the top comes back as soon as the pointer turns. A press on a never-drawn lane lifts it from its resting value the same way.
- **The neighbour snap is measured in travel** (`dragSnapTravel`, 2 px: about 1% of the range, 0.1% with Shift) and applied to the value shown, not the level carried, so it never holds a slow drag back. Alt skips it, as Alt already places freely in time.
- **Unchanged:** time is absolute and grid-snapped, and the click that adds a node lands at the pointer or on the line (`curveSnapDistance`, 5 px on screen). The reading sits beside the dragged node, since a fine drag leaves the pointer some way off.

`AutomationLanes.inc` covers the chooser (placement, a revealed lane becoming the shown one, no mode, the clips' strip, None, a refused own-row lane) and the drag (10 px up is 10/200 of the range on a 30 px lane, Shift a tenth of that, the overshoot, the 1 px snap, Alt). It also renders track 0's row with a ramp on its shown lane as a paint smoke test, written to a PNG when `RHINO_AUTOMATION_TRACK_SNAPSHOT` names a path ([Seeing the UI without taking the screen](headless-ui-snapshots.md)).

## Folding the lanes under a track

Commit `99b4e24`, 2026-10-25, at the user's request. A card whose track has at least one lane on a row of its own carries an arrow in the bottom-right corner of its name side (`automationFoldBounds`, `beginAutomationFoldGesture` in `ArrangementAutomation.cpp`, `paintAutomationFold` in `ArrangementAutomationPaint.cpp`). Clicking it folds those rows away and clicking again brings them back at their height; it points right while folded and down while open, the rule the arrow before the name follows, on a ground of the name's colour at 16% so it reads as a button rather than a second copy of that arrow. A card whose lanes are all drawn over its own clips has nothing under it and no arrow; neither has a row too short to hold it (a hidden group member). Its 7 px margin (`automationFoldMargin`) keeps it clear of the ±4 px bottom edge a card is resized from and of the armed stripe, and `mouseDown` offers it right after the name's arrow, before a press can select or carry the card. The fold is `rhinoAutomationFolded` on the track state (`Session::setTrackAutomationFolded`, `SessionTracks.cpp`), a view setting written without undo and marked `ModelChange::none`, like the row's own fold. The folded rows are laid out at no height (`layoutRows`), the rule a collapsed group's members take, so hit tests and painters skip them with no case of their own, and the lanes stay in the document and go on playing. Folding away a lane that holds the keyboard drops automation focus, or Delete would clear a curve nobody can see. *Show automation on new lane* clears the fold, outside its undo step, so a lane asked for under a folded track is seen; *Show automation*, drawn over the track and visible anyway, leaves it. On a row folded to its one line the arrow shares the name's line and the name stops short of it (`trackNameBounds`). `AutomationLanes.inc` covers all of it and can write PNGs ([Seeing the UI without taking the screen](headless-ui-snapshots.md)).

## How it plays

**The engine plays the lanes** (commit `fe163c8`). `Session::mirrorAutomationToEngine` writes every drawn lane that nobody has taken over by hand onto its parameter's own `te::AutomatableParameter` curve, which the audio graph reads every block, live and in a render alike. The shell's 30 Hz timer used to set each parameter from the message thread instead: values moved in frame-sized steps and stalled whenever that thread was busy, every step rebuilt the interface, and a plain render heard no lanes.

- **When it runs.** `markModified` marks the lanes stale for every change but `ModelChange::none` (a name, a colour, a view setting, which can move no lane; [Session, the model](session-model.md)), and `AutomationMirror`, a listener on the session's own announcement, mirrors them after any such change, leaving unchanged curves alone. It also runs at once when a knob touch takes a parameter from its lane (inside a drag nothing is announced until release) and when an override is toggled. The WAV export calls it before rendering. Since commit `a1c9607` it ends by calling `mirrorClipEnvelopesToEngine`, which writes the clip envelopes' curves.
- **It owns every curve on a track's or the main list's devices.** A clip's own plugin list falls outside this rule: the clip-envelope devices' curves belong to their own mirror. A curve no lane drives is cleared rather than played unseen, whether it came from a document saved with its curves, a device restored by undo or one moved to another track. The edit is searched for strays only when its set of plugins changes, compared by `EditItemID`. Curves are never written through the undo manager.
- **Touching an automated knob overrides it**, as in Live: the knob's **A** button turns amber and the lane stops driving until it is clicked. The override is kept by device key, so it survives a reorder. A lane that stops driving hands its parameter back to the value last set by hand rather than freezing on its final point, unless the user has taken that knob over. Since 2026-10-28 the hand-back is written outside the history. It runs after a command has closed its step, or after an undo, where a write of its own threw away the redo steps and left an unnamed step that the next Ctrl+Z took alone ([Undo and the history](undo-history.md#after-an-undo-or-a-redo)). When the device stores the knob under the parameter's own id, which SDK devices, Rhino EQ and the fader do, the mirror writes that property with no undo manager and calls `updateFromAttachedValue`. Otherwise it falls back to `setParameter`, which is still recorded, so an engine device whose property name differs from its parameter id (the Reverb's "room size" against `roomSize`) still loses its redo steps this way.
- **Moving an automated card bar overrides it too.** The private `Session::takeMixerFromLane` runs from `beginTrackVolumeGesture`/`beginTrackPanGesture` and from `setTrackVolumeDb`/`setTrackPan`, so a double-click reset and the Push's encoders count. If the lane is driving, it marks the runtime overridden and mirrors at once, as `beginDeviceParameterGesture` does for a knob; otherwise the engine's curve pulls the bar back under the hand. *Re-enable automation* calls `toggleParameterAutomationOverride` only after asking whether the bar is overridden, because a toggle chosen twice would take it off again.
- **The rack follows.** The engine tells nobody when it moves a knob, so the Device View reads moving knobs back on its own 30 Hz timer, only while the transport rolls, only for faces with a knob on a lane, and not while hidden ([Device rack and device editors](device-rack.md)). The card bars follow on the arrangement's frame clock instead: `findAutomatedMixers`, at the end of `buildRows`, lists the tracks whose fader or pan an active lane drives, and `followMixerAutomation` reads `Session::trackMixer` for them each frame while the arrangement shows, skipping a bar under the mouse. It sets the bars with `dontSendNotification`: a notifying `setValue` would call `setTrackVolumeDb` and override the lane every frame.
- **Tempo changes rescale every point** by old over new BPM in `Session::setTempo`: points are seconds, and the engine moves clips by beats.

## Limits and traps

- **The mirror holds plugins alive.** `mirroredCurves` keeps a `te::Plugin::Ptr` per curve, so `restoreProject` and `newProject` clear it before the outgoing edit is destroyed. A plugin outliving its edit corrupted the heap (`0xc0000374`) the first time a document was reopened ([Hold ids, not pointers](ids-not-pointers.md)).
- **A parameter is still named by its index** within the device (`parameter`), so a device's parameter order stays persistent ([Stored indices are append-only](append-only-stored-indices.md)). For an external plugin that index is the knob's place in the rack's chosen list, so a knob joins only at the end, and taking one off moves the lanes above it down in the same undo step ([Mapped parameters](mapped-parameters.md)).
- **The main row has no lanes yet.** `showTrackAutomation` refuses a main-track target because the pinned row has nowhere to stack one, although storage (on the edit's own state) and playback already treat it like any track. Its volume and pan bars offer no automation menu either.
- **Undo leaves an override in place.** Undoing the knob move that overrode a lane restores the knob's value, but the lane stays overridden: `AutomationRuntime::overridden` is session state, not part of the document. Live re-enables the automation (open as of 2026-10-28; [Undo and the history](undo-history.md#open-issues)).
- **A latent device's lane is read early.** The engine reads a delayed plugin's curve one latency ahead ([Tracktion reads a delayed plugin's automation early](tracktion-reads-delayed-plugin-automation-early.md)). Clip Pitch corrects for this; track lanes do not (not measured).
