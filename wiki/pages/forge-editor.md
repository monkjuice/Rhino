# Forge editor (panel)

The panel is one `Editor` (`src/ForgeEditor.h`) defined across twenty `.cpp` files: `src/ForgeEditor.cpp` plus `ForgeEditor{Envelope,Filter,Fx,FxView,Input,Layout,Lfo,MatrixView,Modulation,Noise,NoteGuide,OscMode,Pages,Paint,Presets,Spectral,Tables,Theme,Warp}.cpp`. They share the private `src/ForgeEditorInternal.h`. This is the split Rhino uses ([Keeping files small](keeping-files-small.md)); unlike the engine, the panel may span translation units freely ([Forge's engine splits into headers only](forge-engine-headers-only.md)).

## Declared, not computed

`ui/ForgeLayout.h` gathers four headers: `ForgeModule.h` (what a `Control`, `Row` and `Module` are), `ForgeModules.h` (every module, declared once), `ForgePlacement.h` (window limits, where a module lands on the 24-column grid) and `ForgeControlBlock.h` (where a control lands in its row). The editor walks the declarations; no call site computes a cell. The FX rack's slots are the exception: since 2026-10-21 their geometry comes from a per-type strip plan in `ui/ForgeFxStrips.h`, and the declaration only lists their controls ([Forge's FX strips are per-type plans in a pure header](forge-fx-strip-plans.md)). The vocabulary covers the odd cases: `banks` (four envelopes, six LFOs, three racks, two filters, one bank shown at a time), `sharesCell` (two readings of one setting, such as an LFO rate in HZ or BPM), `enabledBy`/`disabledBy`/`modeBy`, and `Seat` for a row placed inside a display or header. A row may declare no controls at all, only to carve a display around a component the editor seats itself: each oscillator's table strip (`Seat::displayTop`, gated to wavetable mode, appended last so no row index moved) is found by `ui::tableStripRow`, and `ui::wavetableTubeBounds` is the display with only its top lowered below it ([Forge oscillators and wavetables](forge-oscillators.md)).

`Module` is an aggregate initialised positionally, so its optional trailing fields (`pages` through `bankEnables`) are reached by counting. Adding or removing one means revisiting every initialiser in `ui/ForgeModules.h` that reaches past it; when `plateName` went (commit `7be9a56`), the six arp panes were the ones easy to miss.

**Bank cards** (since 2026-10-22, when FILTER became two banks; [Forge filter](forge-filter.md)):

- **Per-bank switches.** A module whose banks switch off one by one lists each bank's enable in `bankEnables`, the first being `enableId` (`ui::enableIdFor(module, bank)`). `Editor::showBank` calls `attachEnable` (`src/ForgeEditorPages.cpp`), which re-attaches the header LED and its tooltip to the shown bank's switch. So `module.on()`, the chrome cache key and greying all follow the bank on screen. An empty list means one enable serves every bank, as for the envelopes and the racks. `filterBanksSuite` (`forge_layout`) was proven to fail with the re-attachment removed.
- **Numbers or names.** A card shows a name only when the module's `bankWidth` is wider than the default 32 (`bankButtonWidth`), as the rack's MAIN, BUS 1 and BUS 2 are. The filter's 22 px cards (`filterBankWidth`) therefore stay numbered (`buildBankButtons`, `src/ForgeEditor.cpp`).
- **Title room.** A banked module with no drag handle draws its title and starts its cards past it, at `bankTitleGutterOf(module)` = max(34, 9 px per title letter − 4). Until 2026-10-22 such titles were held to three characters. The layout test now measures the title at the header font (14.5) against that gutter rather than counting letters. At 1180 px wide, FILTER's card "2" ends just short of the part number A-06.

**Adding a control** is its parameter in `src/ForgeParameters.cpp` plus a line in `ui/ForgeModules.h`. The layout test (`tests/ForgeTestsLayout.cpp`) fails if the two disagree in either direction, if a control or enable lacks a real tooltip in `ui/ForgeTooltips.h`, or if a module escapes or overlaps at any allowed size, swept in 20 px steps (about 800 sizes between 1180×820 and 2000×1180). A parameter set by dragging on a display rather than from a cell must be listed in `displayParameters` (`ui/ForgeModules.h`) for that test to find it. As of `bbc2b85` these are the spectral oscillator's four markers, Start, End, LoopStart and LoopEnd ([Forge spectral oscillator](forge-spectral.md)); since 2026-10-13 the LFO module's `lfo{n}Type` and `lfo{n}Direction`, chosen from dropdowns in its display's footer, are listed too ([Forge LFOs](forge-lfos.md)). A parameter whose control is painted on the chassis rather than declared in any module is listed in `chassisParameters()` beside it, and needs a tooltip like any other; since 2026-10-13 that is `arpSlot`, the ARP plate's buttons ([Forge ARP plate, slots and waterfall](forge-arp-plate.md)).

## Pages

OSC, TABLE and MATRIX swap only the oscillator columns; MIX and FX take the whole signal row. Two modules are windows over more rows than they show, the FX rack and, since 2026-10-13, the matrix: both lay their rows at a fixed height from an area translated by the rows scrolled past (`fxScrolledRackBounds`, `matrixScrolledBounds`), hide the rows outside the window in `applyEnableStates`, and draw a thumb with `drawScrollThumb` (`src/ForgeEditorInternal.h`); the matrix also hides the rows past the count the patch has earned ([Forge modulation](forge-modulation.md)). The rack's controls are also children of `fxControlViewport`, a component that clips a partly scrolled row at the window's edge, so their bounds are relative to it: anything that hit-tests or paints over them in the editor's coordinates must map them with `getLocalArea` and ignore points outside the viewport, as `Editor::controlAt` and the drop highlight have since 2026-10-18 (before, a source dropped on a rack knob missed it). The arp overlay is a sixth page, `Page::arp`, that is never a tab ([Forge arpeggiator](forge-arpeggiator.md)), and the note guide a seventh, `Page::guide`, on the same cells ([Forge note guide](forge-note-guide.md)); it is opened from the ARP plate on the keyboard shelf, which is painted on the chassis rather than built of components and is hit-tested in `Editor::mouseDown` before anything a tab owns ([Forge ARP plate, slots and waterfall](forge-arp-plate.md)). Switching hides and shows components instead of rebuilding them, so a hidden knob stays attached, automated and modulated.

## Note guide

Since 2026-10-25 the plate at the keyboard shelf's right-hand end is a door, `ui::NoteGuideDoor`, that names what the guide is set to and opens it as a second overlay, `Page::guide`, on the same ENV and LFO cells the arp's panes take; the arp, the guide and the expanded rack close one another. The panel is a component seated in a declared module with no rows, as the TABLE tab's is. Every C on the keys carries its octave. See [Forge note guide](forge-note-guide.md).

## Title plate and presets

Since 2026-10-19 the title plate names the loaded preset. `ui::PresetPlate` (`ui/ForgePresetPlate.h`) is a component over the cached chassis that shows three lines:

1. The preset's name, letter-spaced, which shrinks before it truncates.
2. `BY AUTHOR / STYLE`.
3. Where the preset came from: its bank folder, or `FROM SERUM 2 / n WARNINGS` in violet for a conversion.

`Editor::refreshPresetPlate` (`src/ForgeEditorPresets.cpp`) re-reads these only when `processor.presetRevision()` moves.

The plate takes no clicks itself; the editor routes them. A click on the name opens the browser, and the rest of the plate's top half is still the theme menu (`onThemePlate`). The plate's tooltip, answered by `Editor::getTooltip`, lists a conversion's warnings.

In Forge the plate sits in `identityTextBounds` on the maker plate. The stamped legend "RHINO FORGE / SYNTHESIZER UNIT 01 / TOKYO-3 AUDIO RESEARCH" was removed from `drawIdentityPlate`, and the hatch now starts at x + 215 (`identityHatchBounds`) to leave the byline room. In Merli the plate sits beside the cat ([Forge themes and Merli](forge-themes.md)). It is drawn unbuffered on purpose ([A buffered child broke Forge's byte-identical paint](buffered-child-breaks-pixel-stability.md)).

The status label beside LOAD (`presetName`, set by `showStatus`) now carries only short-lived messages: errors in violet, or "N THINGS DID NOT CONVERT". The old "INIT SIGNAL" placeholder is gone. The browser that LOAD and SAVE open covers `ui::contentBounds` ([Forge preset browser and bank](forge-preset-browser.md)).

## Rules that bite

- **Visibility is decided only in `Editor::applyEnableStates`.** It runs every 24 Hz tick, so anything hidden or greyed elsewhere is put back next frame; the first FX rack failed exactly so. It is also where a layout that depends on a parameter is noticed: a PATH LFO hangs a "Y" drag card beside its bank card and shifts the bank cards (`src/ForgeEditorLayout.cpp`), and `applyEnableStates` compares the shown type with `lfoPathHandleShown` and calls `resized()` when they differ, so a preset or the host moves the header as a click would.
- **A switched-off module greys everything and hides nothing.** `applyEnableStates` keeps two flags: `inCharge` (`disabledBy`/`enabledBy`, the LFO custom-table Shape rule, `fxKnobLive`) and `on = module.on() && inCharge`. Greying reads `on`; hiding a control in a shared cell (`ui::inSharedCell`) reads only `inCharge`, while `modeBy`/`modeIs` hides the other mode's set. When the hide used `on`, switching an oscillator off emptied the POSITION/SCAN, DETUNE/CUT and BLEND/MIX cells (commit `9b1790a`); the LFO's HZ/BPM rate pair had the same latent fault. `switchedOffControlsStaySuite` in `tests/ForgeTestsLayout.cpp` (CTest `forge_layout`) guards it. When a test counts visible labels, count ones unique to the module (POSITION, DETUNE, BLEND, UNISON, SCAN, CUT): PAN, LEVEL and MIX also label the sub, noise and filter modules on the OSC page.
- **Read values through `Editor::value`**, which asks the parameter rather than the cached atomic beside it. The atomic is updated by another listener, and a panel attachment called first reads the old value: a mode field looked right only after an unrelated click.
- **A picture of what sounds reads `Editor::modulatedValue`; a knob reads `value`.** The matrix never writes a parameter, so a display drawn from `value` stays on the knob while an LFO sweeps the sound. `modulatedValue(id)` (`src/ForgeEditorModulation.cpp`) adds `processor.modulationOffset` in the parameter's own `NormalisableRange`, clamped, exactly as `Core::applyModulation` does. With no note sounding the offset is zero and the picture sits on the knob. Since `2d14aba` (2026-10-13) the oscillator tube's POSITION and the warp depths in `warpStagesOf` read it; the warp mode still reads `value`. Knobs keep `value`, since their live arc already comes from `refreshModulationRings`.
- **Copy, do not point.** `FxSelector` copies a type's choices, because the slot's type can change before the next repaint. The table fields are the exception: their `choices` point into the editor's one `tableEntries` list, so only `rescanTables()` may replace that list, and it repoints all four fields when it does. It runs when the editor opens, when a table menu opens and after NEW or SAVE, never on the 24 Hz tick, where `refreshTableFields` only compares titles (`src/ForgeEditorTables.cpp`).
- **Never give a `juce::Image` static storage.** On Windows it is a Direct2D bitmap, released at DLL unload under the loader lock, where NVIDIA's driver deadlocks; four such statics left RhinoDAW running with no window after it was closed, until commit `f2a972e` (2026-10-22) ([A plugin's static Direct2D images deadlock NVIDIA's driver at unload](plugin-static-images-deadlock-on-unload.md)). An embedded asset is fetched with `ImageCache::getFromMemory` at the point of use, as `fastenerImage` and Merli's `merli::image()` do. An image the panel makes goes through `cachedImage(key, make)` in `ui/ForgeChrome.h` under a key constant of its own beside `grainImageKey` and `wheelImageKey`, and gets a row in `releasedImagesSuite`'s table (`tests/ForgeTestsTheme.cpp`, `forge_theme`), which fails if an image survives once nothing holds it.
- **Colours are theme tokens, applied once.** Every chrome colour is a token in `ui/ForgeTheme.h`, applied by `themeSettings::loadOnce()` (`src/ForgeThemeSettings.h`), which the Editor runs as its first data member, `themeInForce`, declared right after `processor` and before `lookAndFeel` and every control, because controls copy tokens as they are built; a theme change takes effect on restart. Until 2026-10-25 `Processor::createEditor` called `Processor::loadThemeOnce`; the move took the last `ui/` include out of the Processor's header ([Forge's Processor includes nothing under ui/](forge-processor-includes-no-ui.md), commit `ad945e1`). A new chrome colour is a token with a value in every palette, never a literal, and a Merli look goes in as `if (isMerli()) { merli::drawX(...); return; }` at the top of the Forge primitive, leaving the layout and the Forge path alone ([Forge themes and Merli](forge-themes.md)).
- **No knob prints its value, except in the FX rack.** A bubble beside the knob being turned does, so readouts cost no row height. Since 2026-10-21 the rack prints every knob's reading under it at rest (`Editor::paintFxReadings`, `src/ForgeEditorFxView.cpp`). A strip has a line of height to spare for it, and a delay's time or a compressor's threshold is a number you set by reading it. The text is the parameter's own, the same the bubble shows ([Forge mixer and effects racks](forge-mixer-and-fx.md)).
- **A caption that does not fit its box changes font.** A `juce::Label` narrower than its text first squashes the glyphs (default minimum horizontal scale 0.7), then drops the font size, so the word reads as another face; POSITION did in a box its knob's width. So `ui::knobLabelBounds(block, cell)` (`ui/ForgeControlBlock.h`) spans the wider of the knob, fader or rocker block and its cell, and `dressControlLabel` (`src/ForgeEditor.cpp`) sets a `{1, 0, 1, 0}` border with no side padding, without which WARP 1/WARP 2 still overflowed their weight-2 cells at the minimum window. `captionsKeepTheirSizeSuite` (`forge_layout`) checks every caption-size label's string width against its box on OSC, MATRIX, MIX and FX at the minimum and default sizes. It walks visibility flags, because `isShowing()` is always false in a headless editor with no peer.
- **Plates carry no legend.** A plate's foot is only `plateFootMargin` (6 px, `ui/ForgeModule.h`), kept clear of the housing's rail and rivet. The 20 px stamped legend strip (OSCILLATOR A … A-03) was removed in `7be9a56` because it repeated the header, and its height went to the knobs, displays, lower row and mixer strips. Part numbers (B-01…, A-06) stay in the header via `plateCode`; a group such as SUB / NOISE is keyed only by its `group` string, and `drawGroupPlate` draws just the housing.
- **The tick repaints only what moves on its own.** Since commit `ad945e1` (2026-10-25) `Editor::timerCallback` (`src/ForgeEditorInput.cpp`) ends with `repaintLive()`, not `repaint()`. It repaints `Editor::liveRegions()` (public, `src/ForgeEditorPaint.cpp`): the ARP plate, every shown module's header band (`ui::liveHeaderHeight`, 40 px in `ui/ForgeModule.h`, covering the second line GLOBAL and MACROS print on) and its `displayBounds`, the matrix and rack modules whole, and the table panel when visible. When `Editor::liveKey()`, which is `chromeKey(1.0f)`, differs from `liveKeyShown` (a module switched off by a preset, a colour from the host) it repaints the whole panel once instead. Everything else repaints itself when it changes: a knob on a value or ring change, a label on `setText`, the keyboard on its own timer. So anything new that moves without a gesture goes in `liveRegions()` or repaints itself, or it freezes on screen; and anything drawn inside a live region is paid 24 times a second ([Forge's tick repaints its live regions at 24 Hz](forge-panel-repaint-cost.md)). `liveRegionsSuite` (`tests/ForgeTestsDisplays.cpp`, `forge_displays`) paints the panel whole, advances the voices with a chord held and the timer pumped, paints whole again and requires no pixel outside `liveRegions()` to have changed; both frames are painted whole because the rasteriser is not clip-invariant ([JUCE's rasteriser is not clip-invariant](juce-rasteriser-not-clip-invariant.md)).
- **A preset's burst of writes is gated by `processor.isApplyingState()`.** The per-control reactions that lay the rack out or relabel slots (the FX plate's type, the mode selectors, the ModeA/ModeB knobs) and the value bubble return early while `Processor::applySavedState` holds the flag, and `Editor::showPresetResult` (`src/ForgeEditorPresets.cpp`) runs `refreshFxSlots(); resized(); repaint()` once after the load, `resized` running the enable pass; before 2026-10-25 it ran `applyEnableStates()` alone and the rack was laid out once per moved slot. A new reaction that lays out or relabels must check the flag and be covered by `showPresetResult`. Host state restored through `setStateInformation` gets no `showPresetResult`; the tick's `fxTypesShown` comparison catches it ([Forge presets and state](forge-presets-and-state.md#one-way-a-patch-arrives)).

The tick repaints only the live regions and every ordinary knob shares one size: see [Forge's tick repaints its live regions at 24 Hz](forge-panel-repaint-cost.md) and [One knob diameter for the whole Forge panel](forge-knob-diameter-is-panel-wide.md). Lettering is four subset faces chosen by job (`ui/ForgeType.h`); the static metal is drawn by `ui/ForgePanels.h` and cached, and what moves on the ARP plate by `ui/ForgeArpPlate.h`, included from `ui/ForgeVisuals.h`.
