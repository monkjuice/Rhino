# Forge oscillators and wavetables

OSC A, B and C are identical. `oscillatorCount` in `core/ForgeVoiceParts.h` has been 3 since 2026-09-28. Each has:

- its own table
- OCT, SEMI and FINE tuning
- unison of up to 12 voices (`unisonMax`), with DETUNE and BLEND
- PAN and LEVEL
- two warp stages ([Forge warp](forge-warp.md))
- a MODE, either wavetable or spectral ([Forge spectral oscillator](forge-spectral.md))

SEMI and POSITION are continuous so the matrix can sweep them, but a drag by hand snaps SEMI to semitones and POSITION to frames.

## The built-in ten

The built-in table holds `SINE TRI TRAP SQR PULSE THIN SAW HUMP ORGAN VOX` (`core/ForgeShapes.h`). The order is deliberate: POSITION crossfades neighbouring frames, so each frame is a relative of the one before.

`waveShape` fills `builtInWavetable()` once per process and is not on the render path. If a formula change seems to do nothing, the table predates it: restart rather than reload the plugin.

The saw is rotated half a cycle, as Serum stores it, so the display shows its edge. The LFO saw deliberately is not, because an LFO ramp must reset at the cycle boundary.

## Band-limiting

`Wavetable` (`core/ForgeWavetable.h`) keeps 2048-sample frames plus band-limited copies. Each copy is cut by FFT, zeroing the bins above the limit and their mirrors; forgetting the mirrors is the classic bug.

There are three copies per octave (`wavetableLevelsPerOctave`), giving 26 levels and about 50 KB per frame. With one copy per octave, two notes a tone apart could differ by half their harmonics.

The voice reads `levelFor(hz * 1.05 * headroom)` with Catmull-Rom interpolation. The extra 5% allows for the sharpest unison member, and warps add their own headroom. `HANDOVER-M9B.md` and PLAN.md M9b-1 still describe eleven octave-spaced levels and `hz * 1.03` (checked 2026-10-03).

Level 0, the frame as authored, is what the panel draws. The voice reads whichever level the note allows. Do not "fix" the display to match, or it would change with every note.

## Tables from elsewhere

- **The TABLE tab** draws on frames. A stroke rebuilds only its frame (`publishFrame`), and `tableEditSuite` checks the result matches a full `publish`. Undo is the panel's and is lost when the editor closes.
- **Importing a `.wav`:** the frame size comes from Serum's `clm ` chunk, otherwise 2048. JUCE hides that chunk, so the code walks the RIFF. Other sizes are resampled, and tables longer than 64 frames (`maxEditableFrames`) are thinned evenly. Ten 16-frame factory tables in `tables/` are generated by `make-tables.py`.

Tables travel in presets and host state as deflated, base64 `TABLE` nodes ([Forge presets and state](forge-presets-and-state.md)).

## The hand-off

`WavetableStore` (`core/ForgeTableStore.h`) publishes a pointer that the audio thread loads once per block. A counter is odd while a block runs. After a publish, an even counter frees the old table at once, and an odd one parks it. All of it is deliberately `seq_cst`. Never hold a `const Wavetable*` across a publish ([Hold ids, not pointers](ids-not-pointers.md)).
