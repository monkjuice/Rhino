# Session, the model

`rhino::Session` (`native/src/model/Session.h`) is the whole document model: a `juce::ChangeBroadcaster` that owns one `te::Engine` and the open `te::Edit`, and offers every command the UI may issue and every question it may ask of the engine (the transport, the tempo map, undo, the audio device), so no view opens `Session::edit` or `engine` itself ([Reading the document](#reading-the-document)). It runs on the message thread only; the engine owns scheduling, streaming and playback. The one exception is `sendControllerMidi`, which a controller's MIDI thread calls ([Ableton Push 1](ableton-push.md)); a curved keyboard's notes also cross on the MIDI thread, but inside `SessionKeyboards.cpp`'s own port objects, not through a `Session` call. The UI depends on `Session`, never the reverse ([Dependency direction](dependency-direction.md)).

## One class, many files

`Session` is one class defined across 44 translation units, all in `native/src/model/` (the `src/model/Session*.cpp` lines under `# Session` in `native/CMakeLists.txt`, as of 2026-10-27, with `SessionSliceToMidi.cpp` uncommitted at the time of writing; [The source tree mirrors the layers](source-tree-mirrors-the-layers.md)), split by caller as [Keeping files small](keeping-files-small.md) describes:

- `Session.cpp`: construction, the starter edit, `restoreProject`, `projectSnapshot`, undo and redo, `markModified`, the track cache and the relays of the engine's own announcements (`watchEditTree`).
- Notes and patterns: `SessionNotes`, `SessionNoteChop` (since commit `f2ae477`: `chopNotes`, which removes the notes it is given and cuts each `NoteChop` on its own pitch, at the step lines inside it or into equal parts, announced once and opening no transaction of its own, so the note editor's bracket makes a chop adjusted many times one undo step; `gridChopParts`; [Note editor](note-editor.md#gestures)), `SessionNoteTools` (since commit `2220948`: `reverseNotes`, `legatoNotes` and `humanizeNotes` with a seed, Live's Pitch and Time Utilities on the named notes or the whole clip, written through one `placeNotes` that opens no transaction, so the editor's bracket makes each one undo step; [Note editor tools panel](note-editor-tools-panel.md)), `SessionPatternPresets`, `SessionPatches`.
- Tracks and mixing: `SessionTracks`, `SessionTrackClipboard` (copying, pasting, duplicating and removing whole tracks; [Track clipboard](track-clipboard.md)), `SessionGroups`, `SessionMixer`, `SessionReturns` (return tracks, their sends and how mute and solo reach them; [Return tracks and sends](return-tracks.md)), `SessionMeters` (`takeOutputLevels`, what the card meters read; [Track level meters](level-meters.md)), `SessionAutomation`, `SessionTransport`.
- Devices: `SessionDevices`, `SessionDevicePresets`, `SessionDrums` (the [Drum Rack](drum-rack.md)'s kits, pads, banks, slices and drops), `SessionSidechain` (any device's source track and the rule against feedback loops; [Sidechains](sidechains.md)), `SessionExternalPlugins`, `SessionDeviceMacros` (the rack's curated knobs, 4OSC only; `DeviceMacros.cpp` until 2026-10-13), `SessionMappedParameters` (an external plugin's chosen knobs and mapping more from its window; [Mapped parameters](mapped-parameters.md)).
- Clips: `SessionClips`, `SessionRegion` (copy, clear and paste of a region), `SessionRegionMove` (`moveClipRegion`, a selection carried by a clip header or nudged; split off on 2026-10-23 when it took `SessionRegion.cpp` to 630 lines; [Region editing](region-editing.md#moving-a-selection)), `SessionAudioClips`, `SessionClipEnvelopes` (an audio clip's own Gain, Pan and Pitch envelopes, the hidden devices that play them and the fades it holds; [Clip envelopes](clip-envelopes.md)), `SessionClipLoop` (since commit `4a6a17b`, the note editor's Loop switch: `setPatternLooping` sets the open MIDI clip's loop range, which the engine repeats, and `patternStepAt` maps time to the editor's step; [Clip Loop switch](clip-loop.md)), `SessionWarp`, `SessionMerge`, `SessionBounce` ([Bounce to New Track](bounce-to-new-track.md)), `SessionSliceToMidi` (`sliceClipToNewMidiTrack`, an audio clip's slices on a Drum Rack on a new MIDI track; [Slice to New MIDI Track](slice-to-new-midi-track.md)), `SessionExport` (Export WAV, stepped like the bounce; [Project files](project-files.md#wav-export)), `SessionSamples`, `SessionSlots`.
- Input and audition: `SessionRecording`, `SessionMidiInput`, `SessionAudioInput`, `SessionPreview`, `SessionNoteAudition` (since commit `f2ae477`, the note editor's preview: one note at a time injected into the open clip's track on the message thread, its note-off sent to the same track by id; [Note editor](note-editor.md#preview); since `4a6a17b` also `takePatternKeysDown`, the keys that track is playing, read off its hidden DJ input; [Note editor](note-editor.md#keys-the-track-is-playing)), `SessionControllers` (the ports a controller Rhino drives itself reserves, the Push's own input, `sendControllerMidi` and the controller's per-machine settings; [Ableton Push 1](ableton-push.md)), `SessionKeyboards` (a keyboard port's velocity curve, the port Rhino reads to apply it and its stand-in device; [Keyboard velocity curves](keyboard-velocity-curves.md)).
- The DJ booth: `SessionDj` (decks, transport, mixer, what the document saves) and `SessionDjSources` (file and audio-cell reads, MIDI cells made into notes, a deck bound to its track, Live, what an edit leaves a deck to do), sharing `SessionDjInternal.h` ([DJ view and the booth](dj-view.md)). The booth comes off the audio device in `releaseAudioDevice` and the destructor; the destructor and `djReset` unbind every deck from its track first, and `djReset` drains the worker. `ensureTrackMixers` (`SessionMixer`) gives every track the booth's two hidden ends beside its fader ([DJ decks play live through the track's chain](dj-decks-play-live-through-the-track.md)).
- `SessionInternal.h/.cpp`: property identifiers and helpers private to these files, among them `splitClipAt`, `regionTolerance` and `resumePlaybackAfterClipEdit` (promoted from anonymous namespaces on 2026-10-23 when `SessionRegionMove.cpp` needed them) and the render helpers `stopRenderWhenStalled` and `renderTaskOutcome` (the guard that gives up a render with no progress for a given time; the stepped bounce and export call them around their slices), `runRenderTask` (those two around one blocking loop; `renderTimelineWav`, which only tests call, and the slicing's render) and `nextRenderFile` (`<track> <kind> N.wav`; the merge, the bounce and the slicing).

The September 2026 split established the responsibility-based `Session*.cpp` layout; most of the files arrived later.

## What every command does

- **Refuses in the model.** A command that can refuse returns a `juce::Result` whose message the shell prints, so a rule (a track's kind, a bus's limits, chain order) is written once and every UI path inherits it.
- **Makes one undo step per intent.** A named `beginNewTransaction`, the edit, then an empty one to close it. Drags are bracketed (`beginNoteGesture`, `beginAudioClipGesture`, `beginTempoGesture`, the fader, send and device-parameter gestures) so a drag is one entry and one notification, not one per pixel. A fader or send drag's entry restores nothing on undo, which is an engine limit ([Mixer](mixer.md#open-issues)). Inside a device-parameter drag a step announces only on `deviceParameterValues`, and inside a fader or pan drag nothing at all, until the gesture ends. Several clips moved or deleted together are one step (`moveClips`, `deleteClips`), as are several files dropped together (`importAudioFilesAt`) and a moved selection's cuts, clearing and moves (`moveClipRegion`, "Move selection"). View settings (row height, a row's fold, its automation lanes' fold, the lane its row shows, group collapse) skip undo.
- **Fails whole.** A command that fails after it has begun editing takes back its own transaction with `undoCurrentTransactionOnly`, so a refusal half way, after a lane was made or an instrument switched, leaves the document as it was (`editClip`, `moveClips`, `moveClipRegion`, `pasteClipRegion`, `importAudioFilesAt`; commits `b5d17e0`, `d20faa4`).
- **Notifies synchronously** with `sendSynchronousChangeMessage()`, so views re-sync in the same call stack. The view a command was called from may have rebuilt its own cache by the time the call returns: never hold a reference into a view's list across a `Session` call (commit `2df76f8` fixed two in the arrangement). What each announcement costs is [What a change costs the interface](ui-cost-of-a-change.md).
- **Says what it changed.** Every command ends in `markModified(track, ModelChange)`: the track it changed, or -1, and what it changed, a combinable `Session::ModelChange` of `parameters`, `curves`, `notes`, `audioClips`, `devices` and `tempo`, or `none`, with `unknown` (every kind) the default and what undo, redo and the commands that reshape the stack (moving or removing a track, grouping) pass. `none` - names, colours, lane heights, arming, monitoring, inputs, the click, the count-in, the loop range, a group's collapse, a row's fold or its automation lanes' fold, the lane a row shows, mute and solo, the main fader - bumps the revision and the edit's changed flag and nothing else. Anything else marks the lanes stale, so a listener on the session's own announcement writes them onto the engine's curves ([Track automation](automation.md)); and `notes`, `audioClips` or `tempo` leave the DJ decks playing a cell of that track, or every such deck for -1 or a tempo, to make their cell again (`djDocumentChanged`). A device, a parameter or a curve asks nothing of a deck, which hears its track's chain live ([DJ view and the booth](dj-view.md)). A clip edit goes through `markClipsModified`, which marks each track it reached with `notes` or `audioClips` by the track's kind, and with `devices` too where a MIDI clip changed them by bringing its instrument or its kit ([One instrument per track](one-instrument-per-track.md)); a DJ change the document saves goes through `markDjModified`, which is `none`. Adding a track is `none` too: it moves the main row's index, but the mirror finds a curve by its device and parameter, and no deck plays the new track. Every mark also counts against the track it names (`trackRevision`), which the arrangement's per-track clip cache reads, so a command whose result a clip view shows must name its track or pass a kind other than `none` ([Reading the document](#reading-the-document)).
- **Counts its own dirt.** `changeRevision`/`savedRevision` track user commands, because engine initialisation flips the edit's changed flag asynchronously.

Replacing the document (`newProject`, `restoreProject`) is bracketed by `Listener::editWillChange`/`editDidChange`, so views drop cached rows, clips and pointers before the old edit is freed. Anything holding a `te::Plugin::Ptr` must let go before then: the automation mirror's curves and the rack's floating device window both did not, and a plugin outliving its edit corrupted the heap ([Hold ids, not pointers](ids-not-pointers.md)). The meter taps hold one per track, and `releaseMeterTaps` drops them in `newProject`, `restoreProject` and `~Session`. Declaration order destroys the preview and the edit before the engine.

## Reading the document

Added 2026-10-23 ([The 120-track stress audit](stress-audit-2026-10.md)).

- **Track access is O(1).** `audioTracks()` hands back a copy of a cached `juce::Array<te::AudioTrack*>`, `audioTrack(index)` one track (nullptr past the end) and the private `cachedTracks()` the array itself; the single-track getters (`trackCount`, `trackName`, `trackType`, `trackMixer`, `trackRecordInput`, `trackMonitoring`, the group queries and the rest) read it without allocating. Before, each call walked `te::getAudioTracks(*edit)`, 3.7-20 µs, and the arrangement's sync asks about fifteen questions of every track, so a sync grew with the square of the track count: 9-10 ms of getters at 135 tracks. It returns a copy because callers add and remove tracks while holding it (`appendTrack` does). A `TrackCacheWatcher`, a `ValueTree::Listener` on `edit->state`, marks the cache stale when any child is added, removed, reordered, reparented or redirected anywhere in the tree, and the next call rebuilds it: one walk, not one per getter. `watchEditTree()` runs right after an edit is made (`buildStarterEdit`, so `newProject` too) or swapped in (`restoreProject`) and clears the revisions; `unwatchEdit()` runs before `restoreProject` swaps the edit and last in `~Session`, since anything above may still ask for a track. The free helpers in `SessionInternal.cpp`, which take a `te::Edit&`, and `restoreProject`'s check of the candidate edit still call `te::getAudioTracks`.
- **A new `Session` unit asks `audioTracks()`, not `te::getAudioTracks(*edit)`.** The first patch's bulk replace rewrote the cache's own rebuild into a call to itself, and every runner died with 0xC00000FD before it printed a line ([A CTest SegFault may be a stack overflow](stack-overflow-reports-as-segfault.md)).
- **`trackRevision(track)`** counts the changes marked against a track, by its id, plus one count for every track at once. A change that names a track bumps it whatever its kind (a clip's colour is `ModelChange::none` and a view of the track still shows it). One that names none bumps every track unless it is `none`: undo, redo, a tempo change and the commands that reshape the stack bump all, while adding a track at the foot, the loop, the click and DJ state bump nothing. A view that keeps something per track compares the number with the one it kept; equal means the model knows of nothing that changed since. A track not in the stack answers 0.
- **The questions a view asks of the engine** are `Session`'s own: `isPlaying()`, `playheadSeconds()` (the audible position while playing, the transport's own while standing; it was `playheadTime()` in `ui/shared/Playhead.cpp`), `locate(seconds)` (moves the transport and makes the spot the playback start, which a seek in the note editor now does too), `songLengthSeconds()`, `loopRange()`, `secondsToBeats()` and `beatsToSeconds()` (the tempo map), `canUndo()` and `canRedo()`, `restartPlaybackIfRolling()` (Rhino Tune's latency change), `audioDeviceManager()` and `audioDeviceSummary()`, and `ownsDevice(const te::Plugin&)` (a window holding a device closes when the document no longer owns it). They are in `SessionTransport.cpp` and `Session.cpp`. A new need is met the same way ([Dependency direction](dependency-direction.md#the-views-ask-session-not-the-engine)).
- **Two relayed broadcasters, `transportState` and `undoHistory`,** are fed by an `EngineStateRelay` listening to the edit's transport and undo manager, attached in `watchEditTree` and detached in `unwatchEdit`. The shell listens to them, so a new document needs no re-subscription (`MainComponent::editWillChange` is empty). They are apart from the session's own broadcaster on purpose: playing and stopping must not resync every panel.
- **The model asks the pointer nothing.** `gestureHeld()` used to call `juce::Component::isMouseButtonDownAnywhere()`. The shell's 30 Hz timer now tells the model with `setPointerHeld()` just before `djPoll()`, and under the runners nothing sets it, so it stays false.

## Pitfalls

- `isCommandLineTestMode()` suppresses machine preferences (browser preview, the note editor's preview, last track kind, the Push's scale and octave, keyboard velocity curves) and makes `pickTrackColour` deterministic, so a developer's settings cannot change what the suite sees.
- `RhinoEngineBehaviour` is the only thing Rhino tells the engine: where a recording goes, that recording mutes what it covers, and, since 2026-10-12, that a muted track still runs its devices (`shouldProcessMutedTracks`), so the song's mute and solo leave a DJ deck its track ([Mixer](mixer.md)). Since `b7bd73c` it also says whether a send feeds its return while its track is silenced (`shouldProcessAuxSendWhenTrackIsMuted`). The engine reads that only when it builds the graph, so `followReturnSolo` rebuilds the graph when the answer changes ([A send ignores mute while muted tracks are processed](send-ignores-mute-when-muted-tracks-process.md)).
- The newest playing track is `firstReturnTrack() - 1`, not `trackCount() - 1`: the return tracks keep the foot of the stack, and every lane a command makes lands above them ([Return tracks and sends](return-tracks.md)).
- The note editor's clip, `patternClip` kept beside `patternClipID`, is the one long-lived pointer. `repairPatternClip` re-finds it after anything that can take it, and a document with no MIDI track has none, so ask `hasPatternClip()` before `pattern()`. The rest of the positional state from the original pattern-track layout was retired in commit `a04b407` ([No track is special for being first](pattern-track.md)).
- Hold clip ids, not `te::Clip*` ([Hold ids, not pointers](ids-not-pointers.md)).
- A bounce in flight (`isBouncing()`, since 2026-10-14) holds clips and track indices between frames, and an export (`isExporting()`, since 2026-10-15) a render task over the edit, safe only because the shell's modal loading screen blocks input. The model itself guards only `undo`/`redo` (they do nothing while either runs), `newProject`, `restoreProject` and `~Session` (they cancel both first) and the two starts (each refuses while either runs), so any other route that can edit meanwhile must check both ([A long command runs in slices behind a modal screen](long-commands-run-in-slices.md)).
- A device's parameter list can be long: a Drum Rack has 768 controls. On a path that runs per frame or per change, read a range (`deviceParameters(track, slot, first, count)`), one control (`deviceParameter`) or the count (`deviceParameterCount`), not the whole list ([What a change costs the interface](ui-cost-of-a-change.md)).
- View state a command writes without undo may still announce, so other views follow: `showDrumBank` does, for the note editor's drum rows.
- `SessionPatternPresets.cpp` is the [pattern presets](pattern-presets.md); [device presets](device-presets.md) are
  `SessionDevicePresets.cpp`. On 2026-10-05, when it was still `SessionPresets.cpp`, a file write meant to create the
  new one overwrote the old one, restored from git at once; the rename of 2026-10-13 is for exactly that confusion.
  Check a name is free before writing a new file here.
