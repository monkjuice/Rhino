# Track groups (bus tracks)

A group is a bus: a track of its own, with fader, pan, mute, solo and an audio-effect chain, that every member's output feeds. A bus can itself be a member of another group, so groups nest ([below](#nesting)). The model is `native/src/model/SessionGroups.cpp` over the pure rules in `native/src/model/GroupTree.h`; the fold arrow, indented cards, gutter bands and multi-card selection are `native/src/ui/arrangement/ArrangementGroups.cpp`. Why the bus is an ordinary track is [A group is an ordinary bus track](group-is-a-bus-track.md).

## How it works

- **Grouping.** Ctrl+G (`Session::groupTracks`) makes a bus named `Group N`, gives it a `rhinoGroupBus` id one past the highest the document has used (so an id freed by an undo is never reissued), and gathers the chosen tracks under it. A chosen bus brings its whole group. The new group opens where the topmost chosen track was, **inside** whatever group that track was in, so grouping members of a group makes a group inside it. Ctrl+Shift+G (`ungroupTracks`) deletes the bus and hands its direct members to the group around it, or the main output at the top; the tracks survive.
- **Positional structure.** No member list is stored. Each track names the one group it is directly inside (`rhinoGroup`, its *claim*), and where it sits decides whether the claim holds. `reconcileTrackGroups` runs after `addTrack`, `moveTrack`, `removeAudioTrack`, every track paste, duplicate and delete, every group command, every lane a drag or paste makes, and every reopen. It writes back what `reconcileGroupClaims` decides (a plain track carried between two tracks of a deeper open group, or directly under its bus, joins it; a claim on a group not open where the track sits lapses), then routes each track to its claim's bus, top-down so no intermediate step makes a loop (`TrackOutput::setOutputToTrack` for members, the default device for the rest).
- **Adding to and removing from a group.** `addTracksToGroup` keeps the group where it is and appends the newcomers at the end of its run (it used to move the whole group up to the topmost newcomer); it refuses the group itself or any group around it ("A group cannot go inside itself."). `removeTrackFromGroup` moves the track, a bus with its whole group, below what stays and into the parent group, one level up; `removeTracksFromGroup` skips tracks inside a bus that is itself leaving.
- **A moved track** (`moveTrack`) keeps its claim only if it holds at the destination (`claimHolds`); otherwise it is cleared before reconciling, and the track joins whatever group it landed in the middle of. The join rule reads the *next* track's claim, so a stale claim on a track carried away would pull the track above it into the old group. This also fixed an older bug: a member of one group dragged between members of another now joins it, where before its stale claim lapsed and cut the second group's later members loose.
- **A track added under a group** (`addTrack` with `below`, from the `+` menu or Ctrl+T under the selected card; `Session::landingUnder`). It joins the innermost group covering `below`, written explicitly (`trackGroupMemberID`) before reconciling, since under the *last* member is not between two members. Under anything a collapsed group hides (or a collapsed bus) it lands after the **outermost** collapsed group, in that group's parent, since inside a fold it would be made at zero height and never seen. Paste uses the same rule. `TrackManagement.inc` covers it.
- **Bouncing** ([Bounce to New Track](bounce-to-new-track.md), Ctrl+B). A bus card bounces the group after its bus, fader included; the bus is muted and the new track lands under the whole group at unity, outside it, but inside the parent group when the group is nested. A member bounced alone is rendered before its own fader and without the bus ([A track that feeds a bus renders nothing on its own](bus-member-renders-nothing-alone.md)); its new track joins the group by an explicit `trackGroupMemberID`, so it plays into the bus as its source did.
- **Cards on the clipboard** ([Track clipboard](track-clipboard.md)). A bus stands for its group in cut, copy, paste, duplicate and delete. A pasted or duplicated bus is a new group with its own `rhinoGroupBus` id, its copied members (nested groups included) following it. A copied group **nests where it lands** (until 2026-10-27 it always landed below the whole target group); Duplicate lands beside its source at the same level. **Delete on a bus card deletes the whole group** (`Session::removeTracks`), as in Live. Ungroup is the only way to drop just the bus.
- **Undo restores the group whole** (commit `8dd3627`). Membership is written through the undo manager, and every caller reconciles inside the transaction of the move, group or delete that decided it, so one Ctrl+Z puts back position, membership and routing together. Ungrouping and deleting a bus hand the members on and route them while the bus still exists (`dissolveGroupInEdit`). Undo replays a transaction backwards, so it brings the bus back before routing anything into it; routed after the deletion, the members lost their routing for good. After every undo and redo the members' outputs are also re-resolved ([Tracktion resolves a track-to-track output only when its text changes](tracktion-track-output-resolves-on-change.md)). `Arrangement/scenarios/GroupUndo.inc` covers each case.
- **Sidechain loops** (2026-10-26). A group or a move can leave a member's device taking its own group's bus as its sidechain. `reconcileTrackGroups` therefore ends with `clearSidechainLoops`, which clears that source inside the same transaction, so undo restores it ([Sidechains](sidechains.md)).
- **Collapse** is a view setting on the bus (`rhinoGroupCollapsed`, no undo). Members are laid out at zero height rather than removed, so geometry code needs no special case; a track is hidden under **any** collapsed group around it (`isTrackHidden`). The bus's fold arrow stands before its name, as on every card (commit `14c9c77`); on a bus it folds the members and never the bus's own row, on any other card that row alone ([Arrangement view](arrangement-view.md)).
- **Return tracks stay out** (since `b7bd73c`). `groupTracks` and `addTracksToGroup` refuse a return ("A return track stays with the returns, outside any group."), and the card menu hides the group items on one. `reconcileTrackGroups` never joins a return to a group, and before anything else it carries any return found above another track back to the foot of the stack. A bus gets a send to every return, like any other track ([Return tracks and sends](return-tracks.md)).
- **A bus refuses** instruments and MIDI effects (`addDevice`), clips (`createClip`, `importAudioAt`, and since `b5d17e0` moved and pasted ones), clip slots (`clipSlotAt`, `clipLaneRefusal`), arming (`trackRecordInput` answers none) and, since group decks went on 2026-10-12, a DJ deck (`loadDjDeckTrack`).

## Nesting

Since 2026-10-27 (commit `7965c72`), at the user's request ("allow nested grouping. Up to 10 levels").

- **A nested bus** carries both `rhinoGroupBus` (its own id) and `rhinoGroup` (the group around it), and feeds that group's bus like any member: a submix feeding a submix. A group at the top of the stack is level 1; `maxGroupLevels` is 10 (`GroupTree.h`). Plain tracks are not levels, so a track can sit inside a tenth-level group.
- **The limit is refused, not repaired.** `groupTracks`, `addTracksToGroup`, `moveTrack` (checked on a planned stack) and `pasteTracks` (checked before anything lands) refuse with "Groups nest at most 10 levels deep." (`refuseDeepNesting` over `deepestGroupLevel`). `reconcileGroupClaims` only repairs a document that arrives too deep, lifting the offending bus to level 10 beside the group it was in, its members kept.
- **The queries.** `Session::TrackGroup` has `parent` and `level`, and its `firstTrack`..`lastTrack` (so `contains`, `covers`) span every descendant. `trackGroupId(track)` is the group a track is *directly* inside; on a nested bus `trackGroupBusId` is its own group and `trackGroupId` the parent.
- **`GroupTree.h`** is a pure header (no JUCE, no engine), like `ClipGeometry.h`: `GroupSlot {bus, claim, fixed}`, `reconcileGroupClaims`, `readGroupSpans`, `claimHolds`, `deepestGroupLevel`, `withGroupsWhole` (a selection plus everything inside any bus in it) and `gatherTracks` (the block move a group command plans). Session reads the stack into slots (`groupSlotsOf`, `SessionInternal.h`), asks, and writes the answers back. `tests/GroupTreeTest.cpp` checks it on hand-made stacks, in `--arrangement-geometry-test` (CTest `native_arrangement_geometry`).
- **On the cards.** Each level indents a card: `groupIndent` 13 px for level 1, then `nestedGroupIndent` 4 px per further level, 49 px at depth 10, which leaves a name about 22 px with returns present (5 px per level was rejected for that). `paintGroupGutter` paints one band per enclosing group in its colour, outermost at the edge, with a 1 px gap so two default-coloured groups still read as two. `groupContaining` returns the innermost group; `groupDepth` the level.
- **The card menu.** A bus offers Collapse and Ungroup; any card in a group, a nested bus too, offers *Remove ... from group*; any card but a return offers *Group* (Ctrl+G) and *Add to group*, whose submenu indents names by level and leaves out any group a selected bus would have to go inside itself to join.
- **Not done: dragging a bus card moves only the bus**, since `moveTrack` is single-track, leaving its members behind. To nest an existing group, select its bus and press Ctrl+G, or use *Add to group* on the bus card.
- **Tests.** `Arrangement/scenarios/GroupNesting.inc` (its own `Session` and `Arrangement`) covers routing, indent, folds, every command with undo, the limit across group, paste and move, a ten-bus routing chain and the depth-10 card layout. `GroupBusRouting.inc` checks that muting the outer bus silences a member two buses down; `ClipBounce.inc` that a nested group's bounce ignores the outer fader at -40 dB.

## Pitfalls

- **Ask `usesDefaultAudioOut()`, not `getDestinationTrack() != nullptr`.** Deleting a bus leaves its members naming a track that no longer exists. That reads as "no destination" while the engine still resolves it on every render, and an offline render of the edit never returns ([Offline renders that never return](renders-that-never-return.md)).
- **A bus is not made by `appendTrack`.** `groupTracks` calls `insertNewAudioTrack` itself, so a bus has no `rhinoTrackType` (it reads as audio), no Utility device and no picked colour (it draws in a fixed grey until coloured).
- **Skip buses when looking for a playable track**, as the note editor's fallback `firstMidiTrackOf` does; a bus at index 0 once reopened with an instrument on it, silencing the project (`GroupBusReload.inc`).
- **A bus's `rhinoGroup` is the group around it, never its own**, and a top-level bus has none, so a rule that compares neighbours' membership must treat a bus above as opening its group. The join rule did not, and until `8dd3627` a track dropped directly under a bus broke the run and cut every member below it loose. `reconcileGroupClaims` judges a bus by what follows everything it brings (`claimedEnd`), not by its own first member.
- **A member's output names its bus by position, not id**, as the text `"track N"`, resolved only when that text changes ([Tracktion resolves a track-to-track output only when its text changes](tracktion-track-output-resolves-on-change.md)). After a reorder the text is stale while the routing is right, which nesting makes routine; `reconcileTrackGroups` checks both. An edit built from a copy of the state with tracks removed resolves it to the wrong track or none: the DJ bounce's stripped copy routed a member straight to the output, past its bus's fader (2026-10-10). Anything that loads a reduced copy must call `setOutputToTrack` again for every member ([A deck's bounce renders on a worker against a copy of the document](dj-bounce-on-a-copy.md)); nothing builds such a copy now.
- **A member is rendered only as part of its bus.** An offline render naming a member without its bus renders nothing, so every render of one track alone (bounce, merge, slice) wraps itself in `ScopedStraightOut` ([gotcha](bus-member-renders-nothing-alone.md)).
- `migrateLegacyTrackGroups` rebuilds pre-bus documents' groups on load ([No backward compatibility for .rhinoedit](no-rhinoedit-back-compat.md)).
